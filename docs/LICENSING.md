# Licensing and source policy

OpenRepairDB will contain both software and technical knowledge. These are different legal categories and must not be covered by one assumed licence.

## Software

The repository currently has no software licence file. A licence will be selected explicitly before accepting external code contributions. Until then, do not assume that repository contents are licensed for reuse merely because the repository is public.

## Database and original contributions

Before public contributions are enabled, the project must define a clear contributor agreement or contribution terms and a licence for original contributed text and structured data. Images and uploaded documents may need separate terms.

## External sources

For every external source or retained item, record:
- source name and canonical URL,
- author or rights holder where known,
- retrieval date,
- licence identifier and licence URL,
- required attribution,
- whether reuse, modification and redistribution are permitted.

Use the following handling categories:

- **link_only:** retain a citation and link; do not copy the source content.
- **metadata_only:** retain factual metadata only where lawful; link to the original.
- **redistribution_allowed:** retain material only after checking that the licence covers the intended use and attribution.
- **permission_required:** do not republish until permission is obtained.
- **unknown:** default to link-only until checked.

## Important examples

A source being publicly accessible does not mean its text, photographs, schematics or PDFs can be republished. Open licences can also impose conditions such as attribution, share-alike or non-commercial restrictions. Check the exact licence attached to each item, not merely the website's general reputation.

## AI-assisted extraction

AI-generated summaries or extracted facts must retain links to the source evidence. AI output does not remove copyright or licensing obligations. Do not use AI to reproduce substantial portions of restricted source material.

## Takedowns and corrections

Before public launch, provide a contact route for rights holders and users to request correction or removal. Preserve an audit trail for moderation decisions without retaining material that must legally be removed.
